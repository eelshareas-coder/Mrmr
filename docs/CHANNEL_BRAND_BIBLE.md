# Channel Brand Bible v1

## Purpose
Create one recognizable public identity across episodes and platforms.

## Brand promise
Make complex accessibility and communication topics understandable through original cinematic cartoon storytelling, while keeping evidence boundaries visible.

## Audience
Primary:
- families and caregivers
- educators
- people interested in accessible communication and learning

Secondary:
- students and professionals seeking accessible educational explanations

## Voice
- clear
- warm
- respectful
- practical
- evidence-aware
- never sensational about disability

## Visual identity
- original cartoon characters and environments
- cinematic composition
- consistent title cards
- readable Arabic typography
- restrained motion
- accessibility-first captions
- recurring visual motifs

## Editorial rules
Every episode should answer one meaningful question or explain one useful concept.
Avoid diagnosis, individualized treatment plans, cure claims, guarantees, and unsupported promises.

## Platform consistency
The hook, visual identity, terminology, and scientific meaning remain consistent. Length, framing, caption density, and metadata can change by platform.

## Trust markers
Where relevant, show:
- source/evidence note
- educational-scope note
- accessibility-friendly captions
- original/licensed asset status internally

## Brand quality gate
Before release:
- recognizable as part of the series
- understandable without audio
- respectful representation
- scientifically bounded
- rights-safe
- mobile-readable

# Cinematic Cartoon Series Style

## Purpose
Mrmr public content is produced as an original cartoon language presented with cinematic storytelling and serialized continuity.

This is a production style, not a license to copy a named studio, show, artist, or copyrighted character.

## Core identity
- Original 2D/2.5D cartoon visuals.
- Cinematic framing, lighting logic, depth, camera movement, and scene transitions.
- Each episode has a clear beginning, development, and payoff.
- Recurring visual motifs and, when useful, recurring original characters.
- Educational information remains evidence-based and understandable.
- Emotional storytelling may improve comprehension, but must not exaggerate evidence or turn educational content into medical promises.

## Episode grammar
1. Cold open / visual hook.
2. Episode title.
3. Story problem or question.
4. Visual explanation through character action and environment.
5. Evidence boundary when a claim needs qualification.
6. Resolution / takeaway.
7. Short closing card and series continuity cue.

## Visual rules
- Prefer original vector/illustrated assets.
- No identifiable real children or family footage by default.
- No copyrighted screenshots, clips, characters, or imitation of a living artist's distinctive style.
- Character expressions should be respectful and non-stigmatizing.
- Accessibility must survive the cinematic treatment: readable captions, understandable pacing, no meaning conveyed only by color or sound.

## Cinematic rules
- Use shot sizes intentionally: wide for context, medium for interaction, close-up for important details.
- Motion should serve story or comprehension, not distract from the educational point.
- Keep transitions consistent across the series.
- Sound design must remain subordinate to narration and accessibility.
- Every episode should work as a standalone piece while belonging to a recognizable series.

## Quality gate
Before publication, review:
- evidence accuracy
- safety and scope
- dignity/privacy
- rights/licensing
- accessibility
- cinematic clarity
- series continuity
- metadata readiness

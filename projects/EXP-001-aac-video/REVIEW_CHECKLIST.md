# EXP-001 — Review Checklist

## Scientific
- [ ] Every substantive claim traces to the private evidence card.
- [ ] Population/context is not overgeneralized.
- [ ] Uncertainty and limitations are preserved.
- [ ] No single study is universal proof.

## Safety
- [ ] No diagnosis.
- [ ] No individualized treatment plan.
- [ ] No medication advice.
- [ ] No therapeutic guarantee.
- [ ] No unsupported claim that AAC causes/prevents speech.
- [ ] No individual device recommendation.

## Rights
- [ ] Music is original or licensed.
- [ ] Graphics are original or rights-documented.
- [ ] No copyrighted screenshots/clips without permission.
- [ ] Asset manifest is complete.

## Accessibility
- [ ] Captions complete and accurate.
- [ ] Audio understandable.
- [ ] Text readable on mobile.
- [ ] Meaning does not depend on color alone.

## Publish readiness
- [ ] Title approved.
- [ ] Thumbnail concept approved.
- [ ] Description/source list prepared.
- [ ] Short-form derivative identified.
- [ ] Measurement plan recorded.
- [ ] Final export verified.

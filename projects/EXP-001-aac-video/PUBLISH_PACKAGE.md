## Publication gate
Do not publish until:
- scientific review is complete,
- captions are proofread,
- rights/asset manifest is complete,
- final audio/video quality is checked,
- description and source list are prepared.

## Measurement window
Record baseline and first post-publication observations at a defined interval. Do not interpret platform metrics as evidence of clinical or educational effectiveness.

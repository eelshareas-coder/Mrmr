# EXP-001 — AAC Educational Video

## Working title
AAC: فكرة واحدة تساعد على جعل التواصل أوضح

## Audience
Families, educators, and people interested in accessible communication.

## Need
Explain one evidence-supported communication principle clearly without overwhelming the viewer.

## Benefit
The viewer leaves with one understandable principle and a safe way to think about applying it in context.

## Structure
1. Hook: one clear problem and benefit.
2. Explain one concept in plain language.
3. Show a neutral example.
4. State the boundary: general education, not individualized clinical advice.
5. Recap.
6. Invite the viewer to continue to the next evidence-based topic.

## Research requirement
No specific intervention claim is final until the private research review identifies appropriate current evidence and professional/official guidance.

## Production
- Voice: clear, calm, conversational.
- Captions: required.
- Visuals: simple, high readability.
- Format: adaptable to long-form and short-form.
- Assets: original or verified open-license assets only.

# EXP-001 — AAC Educational Video

## Working title
هل AAC يعني جهاز؟ الحقيقة أبسط من كده

## Audience
Families, educators, and people interested in accessible communication.

## Need
Correct the common misconception that AAC means one electronic device, while explaining a single safe principle: AAC can supplement speech or provide an alternative communication mode and should be considered according to the person's needs and context.

## Benefit
The viewer leaves with one accurate, memorable principle and understands that AAC is broader than a device and not a one-size-fits-all prescription.

- Pope, Light & Laubscher 2024/2025 systematic review/meta-analysis.
- 2026 systematic review of communication interventions in minimally verbal autistic children.
- 2025 qualitative systematic review of parent experiences.

## Structure
1. Hook: AAC is not one device.
2. Explain AAC with multiple neutral examples.
3. State that AAC can supplement speech or provide an alternative mode.
4. Explain individualized/contextual selection.
5. State evidence limits.
6. Recap.
7. General-education scope note.

## Production
- Voice: clear, calm, conversational Arabic.
- Captions: required.
- Visuals: original simple graphics; no real child/family footage.
- Format: 60–90 second prototype, adaptable to Shorts/Reels.
- Assets: original or verified open-license assets only.

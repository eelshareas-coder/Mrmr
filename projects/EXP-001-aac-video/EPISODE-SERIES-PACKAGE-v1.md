# EXP-001 — Cinematic Cartoon Episode Package v1

## Series format
**Format:** original cinematic cartoon educational series  
**Episode:** EXP-001  
**Working title:** هل AAC يعني جهاز؟ الحقيقة أبسط من كده  
**Target length:** 60–90 seconds

## Story treatment
The episode opens with a cinematic question: “هل AAC يعني جهاز أو تطبيق؟”

The visual world then moves through a sequence of original cartoon scenes:
1. A character encounters a communication moment.
2. The scene reveals multiple communication modes: gesture, pictures, board, writing, and speech-generating device.
3. The character demonstrates that speech and AAC can coexist.
4. The environment changes to show why context and individual needs matter.
5. A visual “evidence boundary” separates what research supports from what should not be promised.
6. The episode resolves with the idea that AAC is a group of communication supports, not one device.

## Character/visual direction
- Use original fictional characters only.
- No real child or family footage.
- Keep expressions warm, respectful, and non-stigmatizing.
- Use cinematic staging without making disability a source of spectacle.
- Keep the communication tools visually clear and easy to understand.

## Episode continuity
Future episodes can reuse the same original visual language, transitions, title-card treatment, and recurring characters without requiring every episode to tell the same story.

# EXP-001 — Accessibility Checklist

## Audio
- [ ] Speech is clear and paced for comprehension.
- [ ] Background audio never competes with narration.
- [ ] Final mix checked on phone speaker and headphones.

## Captions
- [ ] Full captions included.
- [ ] Captions synchronized and proofread.
- [ ] Captions readable on mobile.
- [ ] Important information is not communicated only through color or sound.

## Visuals
- [ ] High legibility and sufficient contrast.
- [ ] Short on-screen phrases.
- [ ] Visual hierarchy understandable without audio.
- [ ] No flashing or distracting motion.
- [ ] Meaning does not depend on color alone.

## Language and dignity
- [ ] Plain Arabic used.
- [ ] AAC explained at first use.
- [ ] No stigmatizing language.
- [ ] Speaking is not framed as the only successful communication outcome.

## Privacy
- [ ] No identifiable child/family material.
- [ ] No individual case presented as proof.

# Topic Scout & Story Studio Operating Contract v1

## Mission
Continuously supply the company with materially different, evidence-ready episode opportunities and hand them to the writing/production system without collapsing discovery, verification, writing, and approval into one role.

## Topic Scout role

### Responsibilities
1. Scan approved source tiers and discovery channels.
2. Detect new questions, misconceptions, practical problems, emerging tools, and meaningful developments.
3. Produce a Topic Candidate with:
   - topic_id
   - central_question
   - audience
   - category
   - discovery_reason
   - source_records
   - initial_evidence_summary
   - uncertainty
   - sensitivity_level
   - suggested_story_modes
   - originality_notes
4. Compare against recent approved/published episodes.
5. Run the Topic Diversity Gate.
6. Send differentiated candidates to Research & Verification.

### Prohibited actions
- Do not mark a topic VERIFIED.
- Do not turn weak evidence into a factual claim.
- Do not publish.
- Do not bypass diversity, safety, rights, or QA gates.

## Story Studio roles

### Editorial Lead
Turns a verified topic into a clear audience question and story angle.

### Scriptwriter
Creates the original script while preserving verified claims and uncertainty.

### Evidence Editor
Checks claim-to-source traceability and blocks unsupported factual wording.

### Story Editor
Checks pacing, narrative coherence, originality, and whether the story is materially different from recent episodes.

### Storyboard/Visual Director
Converts the approved story into shot-level production instructions and visual metaphors.

## Production House roles

### Asset/Character Director
Maintains the original visual system and asset provenance.

### Voice/Audio
Produces or manages original narration, sound, and accessible audio packaging.

### Animation/Edit
Builds the final episode from approved assets and storyboard.

### Accessibility/QA
Checks captions, readability, pacing, audio/visual redundancy, technical export, safety, and rights.

### Distribution
Prepares metadata, thumbnail, upload package, and publication record. It must never claim publication without an external publication record.

## Executive Brain

The General Manager coordinates all departments and makes the production-priority decision after verification. The GM may select among verified candidates, request redesign, pause work, or redirect resources, but cannot override mandatory safety, evidence, rights, accessibility, QA, or publishing controls.

## Pipeline handoff

TOPIC_SCOUT
→ TOPIC_DIVERSITY_GATE
→ RESEARCH
→ VERIFICATION

→ STORY_STUDIO

→ QA

→ ANALYTICS

## Success condition
A healthy company continuously creates a queue of distinct, evidence-ready topics while keeping writing, production, distribution, and executive decisions connected but independently gated.

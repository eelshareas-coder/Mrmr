## Current platform limitation
EXP-001 has no real account connection and no final video export yet. The system must therefore stop at PUBLISH_READY and never claim a publication that has not been externally confirmed.

## Monetization design principle
Automation is used for research, orchestration, testing and production assistance; every episode must remain materially different in substance, story, focus and educational value. YouTube's current monetization guidance says repetitive or mass-produced content can be ineligible, while a recurring character series can be eligible when each video has a distinct storyline, focus or concept. See the official YouTube monetization policy.

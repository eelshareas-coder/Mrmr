# Research & Verification Protocol v1

## Goal
Turn a candidate topic into a traceable evidence package for editorial decision-making.

## Workflow
DISCOVER → COLLECT → SCREEN → CROSS-CHECK → SUMMARIZE → LIMITATIONS → VERIFY → EPISODE_BRIEF.

## Evidence requirements
For every factual claim intended for the episode:
- record source;
- record publication/update date when available;
- distinguish direct evidence from interpretation;
- record uncertainty or disagreement;
- avoid overstating causation.

Sensitive claims require stronger verification and conservative wording.

## Output
The researcher produces:
- Evidence Card;
- Claim Ledger;
- Source Ledger;
- Uncertainty Notes;
- Recommended audience framing;
- Red-flag claims requiring additional review.

No episode enters production as VERIFIED without a complete evidence package.

# Story Engine v1

## Purpose
Convert an approved Episode Brief into a materially distinct story, script, storyboard contract, and production plan while preserving the evidence boundary.

## Core rule
Recurring characters and visual language are allowed. Episode substance must materially vary in central question, conflict/problem, story mode, character goal/action, visual metaphor, evidence set, takeaway, and scene progression.

YouTube's current monetization guidance says a series can use recurring characters when each video has a distinct storyline, focus, or concept, while repetitive or mass-produced content can be ineligible for monetization. citeturn0search0turn0search1

## Inputs
- verified Episode Brief
- evidence card and claim ledger
- originality check
- character registry
- rights constraints
- accessibility plan
- target duration

## Story-mode selector
Choose one primary mode:
1. investigation
2. school/community situation
3. family situation
4. myth/fact
5. assistive-tech demonstration
6. discovery adventure
7. dialogue/problem-solving
8. day-in-the-life fictional scenario

Selection must be justified by the educational objective.

## Story contract
Required outputs:
- hook
- inciting problem/question
- character objective
- discovery/progression beats
- evidence integration points
- uncertainty/evidence boundary
- resolution
- takeaway
- accessibility beats
- rights-sensitive asset notes

## Script rules
- Claims must remain inside the verified evidence boundary.
- Never convert a single study into a universal promise.
- Keep educational claims traceable to the claim ledger.
- Use plain language without removing important uncertainty.
- Do not diagnose or prescribe individualized medical/psychological treatment.
- Avoid disability as spectacle, pity, humiliation, or a punchline.
- Fictional demonstrations are not evidence about real individuals.

## Originality test
Compare each candidate episode against recent published/approved episodes on question, problem, story mode, takeaway, evidence base, visual metaphor, character objective, and scene rhythm.

If similarity is high across several dimensions, BLOCK and request a new story concept.

## Output contract
story-package.json must contain:
- episode_id
- selected_story_mode
- story_rationale
- hook
- inciting_problem
- character_objective
- beats
- evidence_integration
- evidence_boundary
- resolution
- takeaway
- accessibility_beats
- rights_notes
- originality_check
- approval_state

## Human/managerial gate
The Story Engine may propose. It does not self-authorize publication. Executive approval remains with the configured decision hierarchy, with Mahmoud as failover decision lead.

# Episode Brief Schema v1

A verified topic is converted into a production contract.

Required fields:

- episode_id
- working_title
- core_question
- audience
- category
- evidence_card
- primary_sources
- secondary_sources
- key_claims
- uncertainty_and_boundaries
- misconception_or_problem
- selected_story_mode
- visual_metaphor
- protagonist_action
- team_roles
- educational_takeaway
- accessibility_plan
- rights_plan
- monetization_safety_check
- metadata_intent
- originality_check
- approval_state

## Story-mode selection
The production planner selects the mode that best serves the subject:
- investigation;
- school/community situation;
- family situation;
- myth/fact;
- assistive-tech demonstration;
- discovery adventure;
- dialogue/problem-solving;
- day-in-the-life fictional scenario.

The recurring cartoon girl and team may return, but the central story and educational substance must materially change between episodes.
